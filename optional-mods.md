# Optional Mods

These are **optional client-side mods**. They change your personal experience — visuals, HUD, controls, and convenience — and do not affect the server or other players. Tick the ones you want; skip any you don't.

> Mods not listed here (content, worldgen, and library mods) are **required** and installed automatically, because they must match the server.

---

## Performance & FPS

- **Sodium** — `sodium-fabric-0.8.12+mc26.1.2.jar` — Modern rendering engine that dramatically improves frame rate and reduces stutter.
- **Sodium Extra** — `sodium-extra-fabric-0.8.7+mc26.1.1.jar` — Adds extra options to Sodium (fog, particles, FPS display, and more toggles).
- **Reese's Sodium Options** — `reeses-sodium-options-fabric-2.0.5+mc26.1.1.jar` — A cleaner, redesigned settings menu for Sodium.
- **FerriteCore** — `ferritecore-9.0.0-fabric.jar` — Reduces memory (RAM) usage, leading to fewer lag spikes.
- **Entity Culling** — `entityculling-fabric-1.10.2-mc26.1.jar` — Skips rendering entities that are hidden behind blocks, boosting FPS.
- **Iris Shaders** — `iris-fabric-1.10.9+mc26.1.1.jar` — Adds shader support so you can run shader packs alongside Sodium.

---

## Lighting & Visuals

- **Gamma Utils** — `Gamma-Utils-3.0.0+mc26.1.jar` — Full-brightness and gamma control; lets you brighten the world beyond vanilla limits.
- **LambDynamicLights** — `lambdynamiclights-4.10.2+26.1.2.jar` — Dynamic lighting — held and dropped light sources (torches, glowstone, etc.) actually emit light as you move.

---

## HUD & Information

- **Jade** — `Jade-mc26.1-Fabric-26.1.0.jar` — Shows information about the block or mob you're looking at (name, mod, durability, etc.).
- **AppleSkin** — `appleskin-fabric-mc26.1-3.0.9.jar` — Displays detailed hunger and saturation information on the food bar.
- **Shulker Box Tooltip** — `shulkerboxtooltip-fabric-5.2.18+26.1.jar` — Preview the contents of a shulker box just by hovering over it.
- **Enchantment Descriptions** — `EnchantmentDescriptions-fabric-MC26.1.2-26.1.2.3.jar` — Adds tooltips explaining what each enchantment actually does.
- **Just Enough Items (JEI)** — `jei-26.1.2-fabric-29.6.2.31.jar` — Recipe and item lookup; search any item and see how to craft or obtain it.
- **MiniHUD** — `minihud-fabric-26.1.2-0.39.5.jar` — Customizable on-screen overlay (chunk borders, coordinates, spawn info, and more).
- **Detail Armor Bar Reconstructed** — `DetailArmorBarReconstructed-5.2.1+26.1-SNAPSHOT-fabric.jar` — Adds armor type and durability detail to the armor HUD bar.
- **Leave My Bars Alone** — `LeaveMyBarsAlone-v26.1.0-mc26.1.x-Fabric.jar` — Stops the health and hunger bars from jittering or shifting around.

## Camera & Recording

- **Camera Utils** — `camerautils-fabric-1.1.2+26.1.2.jar` — Camera tweaks like freezing the camera and adjusting perspective.
- **Freecam** — `freecam-fabric-1.4.0-rc.1+mc26.1.2.jar` — Detach your camera and fly around freely to view your surroundings.
- **Flashback** — `Flashback-0.40.0-for-MC26.1.jar` — Powerful replay and recording tool for capturing and editing cinematic gameplay. *(Large download.)*

## Quality of Life

- **Controlling** — `Controlling-fabric-26.1.2-26.1.2.4.jar` — Adds a search bar to the keybinds menu so you can quickly find and fix controls.
- **Auto Pickup** — `autopickup-fabric-1.4.12+26.1.x.jar` — Automates item pickup and inventory handling.
- **CutThrough** — `CutThrough-v26.1.0-mc26.1.x-Fabric.jar` — Lets you continue mining or attacking smoothly through to the next target.
- **Scribble** — `scribble-2.0.2+mc26.1.2.jar` — Enhanced book editing with text formatting and extra options.
- **Immersive Hotbar** — `immersive-hotbar-1.1.1-26.1.jar` — Automatically hides the hotbar when idle for a cleaner screen.
- **Chat Animation** — `chatanimation-fabric-26.1-1.1.3.jar` — Smoothly animates incoming chat messages.
- **Smooth GUI** — `smoothgui-fabric-26.1-1.1.1.jar` — Adds smooth animations and transitions to menu screens.

## Interface Customization

- **Mod Menu** — `modmenu-18.0.0-beta.1.jar` — In-game menu to view installed mods and access their settings.
- **Tweakeroo** — `tweakeroo-fabric-26.1.2-0.28.4.jar` — A toolkit of small client-side tweaks and conveniences (free placement, auto-tools, and more).
